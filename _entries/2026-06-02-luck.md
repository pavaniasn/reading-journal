author: "Paul Graham"

summary: |
  Graham argues that luck is not purely random but something you can position yourself for. The more curious you are, the more you read, connect, and try, the larger your surface area for luck to land on. The essay is short but the idea is one of those that reframes how you think about agency and chance.
notes: |
  Took this into a talk with a thousand students. The line "make yourself a big target for luck" unlocked a longer question for me: when good things happen, do we give ourselves enough credit for having positioned ourselves there? And does that question even have the same answer for everyone, depending on where they start?
