source: "Public Administration concept · Class session"

summary: |
  Miles' Law: "Where you stand depends on where you sit." The position you occupy in a system shapes what you see, what you prioritize, and what you advocate for. It's not about integrity or honesty — it's about vantage point. Two equally honest people sitting in different roles will reach different conclusions from the same evidence.
notes: |
  This stayed with me the whole day because it kept showing up in every other idea I encountered. The founder and the executive are both "right" — they just sit in different seats. The same budget, the same organization, the same facts. Different seats, different stands.
